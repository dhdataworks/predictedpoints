How the number is built

Each player's per-90 expected score is assembled from the 2026/27 scoring rules rather than from last season's points total. Goals come from expected goals, assists from expected assists, clean sheets from a Poisson draw on expected goals conceded, and defensive contribution from the chance of clearing the 10 or 12 action threshold. Bonus and cards use last season's observed rate.

That per-90 figure is then multiplied by expected minutes, discounted for injury or suspension, and pushed up or down by team strength and the difficulty of the fixtures in the window you've chosen. Click any row to see the decomposition.

Players with no Premier League minutes at all have nothing to decompose. They are marked est and given the median for their position and cost bracket, which is a guess rather than a projection.
